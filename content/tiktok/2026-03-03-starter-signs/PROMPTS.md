# Gemini Imagen 3 Prompts — Video 1: Starter Signs

**Style note:** All images should be photorealistic, warm tones, rustic kitchen aesthetic, soft natural light. Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio (1080x1920). Leave space in the bottom third for text overlay.

---

## Frame 1 — Hook (active starter close-up)

```
Photorealistic close-up of a glass jar filled with active sourdough starter, many bubbles visible through the glass walls, the starter has risen and is puffy. Warm rustic kitchen background slightly blurred. Soft golden morning light from a window. Shot from eye level. Vertical 9:16 composition with empty space in the lower third for text overlay. Food photography style, shallow depth of field.
```

## Frame 2 — Bubbles (top-down active starter)

```
Photorealistic top-down overhead view of an open glass jar of sourdough starter at peak activity, surface covered in many small and large bubbles, airy and alive-looking. Rustic dark wooden counter beneath. A wooden spoon rests nearby. Warm natural light. Vertical 9:16 composition with space in the lower portion for text. Food photography, sharp focus on bubbles.
```

## Frame 3 — Hooch (dark liquid layer)

```
Photorealistic close-up of a glass jar of sourdough starter with a visible dark grey-brown liquid layer (hooch) sitting on top of the starter. The liquid is slightly separated from the dough beneath. Kitchen background with warm tones, slightly out of focus. Vertical 9:16 composition with space at bottom for text overlay. The image should look slightly concerning but not disgusting. Moody lighting.
```

## Frame 4 — Smell (person reacting)

```
Photorealistic close-up of a young woman with brown hair leaning slightly toward an open glass jar of sourdough starter on a kitchen counter, her nose slightly wrinkled in a curious expression, one hand holding the jar. Warm kitchen background with wooden shelves. Soft natural window light. Vertical 9:16 composition with space at bottom for text. Lifestyle food photography, candid feel.
```

## Frame 5 — CTA (beautiful active starter)

```
Photorealistic beauty shot of a glass jar of sourdough starter with a rubber band around it marking the previous level, the starter has risen well above the band. Golden warm morning light streaming from the side. Clean minimal kitchen background, slightly blurred. The jar is the hero element, centered. Vertical 9:16 composition with ample space in the lower third for text overlay. Aspirational food photography, warm and inviting.
```
