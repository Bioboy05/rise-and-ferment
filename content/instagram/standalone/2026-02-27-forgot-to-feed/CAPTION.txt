I forgot to feed my starter for 3 days.

I opened the jar expecting the worst.

Grey liquid on top.
No bubbles.
A smell that made me step back.

I thought it was dead.

It wasn't.

That grey layer? It's called hooch. It's just alcohol from hungry yeast. Not mold. Not death. Just your starter whispering: "I'm still here. Feed me."

Here's how I brought it back in 48 hours:

1. Poured off the hooch
2. Kept just one tablespoon of starter
3. Fed 1:1:1 (starter : flour : water)
4. Waited 12 hours
5. Fed again

Second feeding — bubbles came back.
Third feeding — doubling like nothing happened.

Your starter has survived worse than your busy week.

So if you're staring at a neglected jar right now feeling guilty — stop. Just feed it. It'll forgive you before you forgive yourself.

Save this. You'll need it someday.
