I forgot to feed my starter for 3 days. Here's what happened.

Life got busy. I had a deadline, then a friend's birthday, then one of those evenings where you sit on the couch and forget you're a person who bakes.

By day 3, I remembered. Ran to the kitchen. Opened the jar.

Grey liquid on top. No bubbles. A smell that was... not great.

I thought it was dead. I genuinely considered a small funeral.

But here's what nobody tells you: sourdough starters are incredibly resilient. That grey liquid? It's called hooch — it's just alcohol from hungry yeast. It's not mold. It's not death. It's your starter saying "hey, I'm still here, but I'd really like breakfast."

What I did:
1. Poured off the hooch
2. Scooped out a small amount of starter (about a tablespoon)
3. Fed it fresh flour and water (1:1:1 ratio)
4. Waited 12 hours
5. Fed again

By the second feeding, bubbles were back. By the third, it was doubling like nothing happened.

Your starter has survived worse than your busy week. Trust me.

So if you're staring at a neglected jar right now, feeling guilty — don't. Just feed it. It'll forgive you faster than you think.

Save this for the next time life gets in the way.
