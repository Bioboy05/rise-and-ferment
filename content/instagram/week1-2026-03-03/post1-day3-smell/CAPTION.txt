Day 3. The smell hits you. You open the jar and genuinely wonder if something has gone wrong.

I've been there. Twice. The first time, I threw it away. The second time, I kept going.

That second time gave me bread.

Here's the thing nobody tells beginners: Day 3 is supposed to smell terrible. It's not a sign of failure — it's a sign of life. The bad bacteria are dying off. The good ones are winning. You're witnessing a tiny war inside a jar, and the right side is winning.

Your only job? Keep feeding. Same time, every day. 50g flour, 50g water, stir, cover loosely, wait.

By Day 5-7, you'll notice the smell shifts. From "gym locker" to "tangy yogurt." That's when you know you're on the right track.

If you're on Day 3 right now and thinking about giving up — don't. You're closer than you think.
