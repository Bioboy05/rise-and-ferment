My entire sourdough setup cost less than a Friday night out.

When I started baking, I went down a rabbit hole of "essential equipment" lists. $200 Dutch ovens. $50 proofing baskets. Specialty flours. Danish dough whisks. I almost convinced myself I needed a $400 stand mixer before I'd even made my first loaf.

Here's what I actually use, every single week:

1. Glass jar — for my starter (any jar works, mine was a jam jar)
2. Digital scale — ~$12. Non-negotiable. Baking by weight changed everything
3. Cast iron Dutch oven — ~$35. Creates the steam your bread needs
4. Banneton basket — ~$14. Gives that beautiful spiral pattern
5. Bread lame — ~$9. Score your bread like a pro

Total: under $75. Under $65 if you skip the banneton.

That's it. Everything else is optional. You don't need the expensive mixer. You don't need the imported flour. You don't need the $169 proofing box (though I'll admit, I upgraded eventually and I love it).

Start simple. Upgrade when you know what you actually need.
