I built the app I wish I had when I started.

When I was keeping my first sourdough starter alive, I had:
- Sticky notes on the fridge
- 14 browser tabs open
- 3 different Reddit threads bookmarked
- A notebook full of "wait, when did I feed it last?"

Rise & Ferment puts everything in one place:

- Starter health tracker (is it alive? yes, yes it is)
- Feeding reminders (because life gets busy)
- Day-by-day guides (no guessing, ever)
- Built-in recipes with ingredient calculator
- Baking planner that works backwards from when you want bread

It's free. It works on your phone. And it was made by someone who killed their first starter on Day 3.
