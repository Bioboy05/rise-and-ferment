```
shot on Canon 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field,
```

**Pasul 2 — Creează Character Sheet (3-4 unghiuri):**
Selectează imaginea preferată, apoi generează variante:
```

hands kneading dough, same kitchen, same clothing, 
soft natural light --ar 4:5 --style raw
```

```
[imagine referință ca Character Reference]

flour dust on her cheek, holding a glass jar with

```

**Pasul 3 — Salvează 4-6 imagini de referință = Identity Pack**
Acestea devin "ancora" pentru toate generările viitoare.

**Pasul 4 — Generează scene specifice:**
```
[Character Reference locked] 
Woman shaping sourdough dough on a floured wooden board, 
hands covered in flour, concentration on face, kitchen 
background blurred, warm morning light, editorial food 
photography --ar 4:5 --style raw
```

**Tips critice Midjourney V7:**
- Folosește ÎNTOTDEAUNA Character Reference (nu Image Prompt)
- Păstrează seed-ul pentru consistență extra (~15-20% mai puțin drift)
- Schimbă O SINGURĂ variabilă pe prompt (lumina SAU unghiul SAU acțiunea)
- Folosește `--style raw` pentru look natural (nu stylizat)
- `--chaos` sub 10 pentru consistență facială
- `--ar 4:5` pentru Instagram, `--ar 9:16` pentru Stories/Reels/TikTok

## 4. CONTENT PILLARS — Ce postezi

### Pillar 1: 🫙 Starter Life (40% din content)
Imagini cu Elena + borcane cu maia în diverse stadii.

**Exemple de posturi:**

**Post 1 — "Day 3 Panic"**
- Imagine: Elena ținând un borcan cu starter la nivelul ochilor, privind sceptic
- Caption: "Day 3 of your starter and nothing is happening? GOOD. That weird smell? Normal. Those tiny bubbles? They're the beginning of something amazing. Here's what's actually happening inside your jar right now... 🧵

Day 1-2: Almost nothing visible. The wild bacteria from the flour are just waking up.
Day 3-4: The BIG rise and fall. This tricks everyone into thinking it's ready. It's NOT. This is leuconostoc bacteria — they're temporary visitors.  
Day 5-6: Quiet phase. The real heroes (lactobacillus) are taking over. Trust the process.
Day 7+: Consistent, predictable rises. THIS is your starter.

The #1 mistake? Giving up on Day 4 when it stops rising. That's exactly when the magic is starting.

✨ AI-illustrated | Real sourdough science
🫙 Track your starter's journey → link in bio"

---

**Post 2 — "The Float Test Truth"**
- Imagine: Elena cu o lingură deasupra unui pahar cu apă, starter plutind
- Caption: "The float test isn't what you think. 🫙

Yes, if a spoonful of starter floats in water, it means there's enough gas trapped inside. But here's what nobody tells you:

→ A starter can PASS the float test and still not be ready
→ A starter can FAIL the float test and still make great bread
→ The real indicator? Consistent doubling in 4-6 hours at 24°C

The float test is a clue, not a verdict. Your starter's RHYTHM is what matters.

✨ AI-illustrated | Real sourdough science"

### Pillar 2: 🍞 Bread Making (30% din content)
Elena în diverse stadii ale procesului de panificație.

**Exemple:**

**Post 3 — "The Windowpane Test"**
- Imagine: Elena întinzând aluat transparent între degete, lumina trecând prin el
- Caption: "This is how you know your dough is ready. 

Stretch a small piece thin enough to see light through it. If it tears? More kneading (or more time for autolyse).

But here's the real secret: 80% of your bread's success is decided BEFORE you start kneading. It's in:
→ Your starter being at peak (not before, not after)  
→ Water temperature (25-30°C, not random tap water)
→ Flour protein content (minimum 11% for good structure)

Get these 3 right and even a messy technique makes beautiful bread.

✨ AI-illustrated | Real sourdough science"

---

**Post 4 — "Autolyse Magic"**
- Imagine: Elena acoperind un bol cu aluat cu un prosop umed
- Caption: "The laziest technique that makes the BIGGEST difference.

Autolyse = just mix flour + water. Wait 30 minutes. That's it.

What happens in those 30 minutes:
🔬 Flour absorbs water fully (better hydration)
🔬 Gluten starts forming WITHOUT kneading
🔬 Enzymes break down starches (better flavor)

Result: Your dough practically kneads itself. Less work, better bread.

I add my starter AFTER autolyse. Some people add it during. Both work. Try both, see what YOUR dough prefers.

✨ AI-illustrated | Real sourdough science"

## 5. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC STRATEGIES

### Instagram (@riseandferment)

**Format mix:**
- 3 feed posts/săptămână (imagini statice Elena + tips)
- 5 Stories/săptămână (quick tips, polls, behind-the-scenes)
- 1 Reel/săptămână (process shots, time-lapses)
- 1 Carousel/săptămână (educational deep-dive)

**Posting schedule:**
- Luni: Feed post (Starter tip)
- Marți: Story (Poll: "Which flour do you use?")
- Miercuri: Carousel (Science bite)
- Joi: Story (Quick tip)
- Vineri: Feed post (Bread making)
- Sâmbătă: Reel (Process video)
- Duminică: Feed post (Community/relatable)

**Hashtag strategy:**
Primary (always): `#sourdoughstarter #sourdoughbread #homemadebread #riseandferment`
Secondary (rotate): `#sourdoughtips #breadbaking #artisanbread #sourdoughjourney`
Branded: `#AIillustrated #RealSourdoughScience`

---

### TikTok (@riseandferment)

**Format:** Short-form video, 15-60 secunde

**Content types:**
1. **"Did you know?"** — Quick sourdough facts cu text overlay pe imagini Elena
2. **"Stop doing this"** — Myth-busting (ex: "Stop throwing away your discard!")
3. **"Starter check"** — Imagini/video cu borcane de maia în diverse stadii
4. **"Silent process"** — ASMR-style bread making cu muzică trending

**Posting:** 1 video/zi (TikTok recompensează frecvența)

**TikTok-specific tips:**
- Folosește trending sounds
- Hook în prima secundă ("The reason your bread is flat isn't what you think")
- Text overlay pe fiecare frame
- Vertical format ONLY (9:16)

---

### X / Twitter — STRATEGIE SPECIALĂ

**Abordarea pentru X este fundamental diferită.**

X nu e loc de imagini frumoase. X e loc de **KNOWLEDGE THREADS** și **HOT TAKES**.

**Formatul killer pe X:**

**Thread-uri educaționale (1/săptămână):**
```

3/ The "hooch" (brown liquid on top) isn't mold. 
It's alcohol. Your starter is hungry, not dying. 
Feed it and move on.

4/ You don't need to feed your starter every day. 
Fridge it. Feed once a week. It'll be fine. 
Your starter is tougher than you think.

15/ If you want to track all of this automatically,
```
