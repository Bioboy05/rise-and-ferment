## Caption Formula (Retention-Engineered)

Fiecare caption urmeaza structura:

1. **Hook** (linia 1) — Curiositate pura, opreste scroll-ul
2. **Tensiune** (liniile 2-5) — Construieste drama, scurte, line-by-line
3. **Twist** (mijloc) — Momentul "aha" sau surpriza
4. **Autoritate subtila** — Explica DE CE, nu doar CE
5. **Pasi actionabili** — Liste numerotate (save-bait)
6. **Empatie** — "Daca si tu..." — permisiune, nu judecata
7. **CTA final** — "Save this", "Tag someone", "Link in bio"
8. **Dot spacers** — 3 puncte inainte de hashtag-uri
