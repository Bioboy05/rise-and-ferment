# Instagram Strategy — Rise & Ferment

**Date:** 2026-02-26
**Approach:** "Elena's Journal" — everything through Elena's warm, relatable voice

---

## Brand Voice

Elena: early 30s, Eastern European, warm, honest, slightly self-deprecating. Never preachy, never salesy. Like a friend texting you baking tips.

**Tone examples:**
- "Day 3 and your starter smells like nail polish? Don't panic. I've been there."
- "The first loaf is never perfect. And that's exactly the point."
- "I started with a jam jar and a tea towel. Here's what I actually use now."

---

## Content Pillars (rotating weekly)

| Pillar | Day | Format | Goal |
|--------|-----|--------|------|
| **Teach** | Monday | Carousel (5-7 slides) | Education — tips, science, myth-busting |
| **Inspire** | Wednesday | Single image + caption | Motivation — beginner wins, encouragement |
| **Gear** | Friday | Single image or reel | Affiliate — "what I actually use" tool spotlights |
| **App** | Saturday | Screen recording / reel | Showcase — app features, walkthroughs |

---

## Visual Style

- **Aesthetic:** Warm, moody, rustic — cozy kitchen at golden hour
- **Color palette:** cream #FFFBF5, crust brown #8B5A2B, warm accents
- **Photography style:** Canon EOS R5, 85mm f/1.4, natural window light
- **Elena:** Light brown wavy hair, cream linen apron, earth-tone top, flour-dusted hands
- **Instagram ratio:** 4:5 portrait (1080x1350px)
- **Midjourney:** Always use `--style raw` for realism, `--ar 4:5` for IG
