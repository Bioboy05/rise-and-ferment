## SOURDOUGH STARTER TEXTURE REFERENCE (for judging Midjourney output)

Real active starter looks like:
- **Consistency:** Thick pancake batter — creamy, not solid, not liquid
- **Color:** Off-white to light beige (with white flour), matte with slight sheen
- **Bubbles through glass:** Honeycomb network of MIXED sizes (mostly tiny 1-2mm, some medium 3-5mm, rare large 8-15mm)
- **Surface:** Subtle dome shape at peak, small bubbles breaking on top
- **Jar marks:** Dried tide-lines on glass above current level (where it rose and fell)
- **Jar size:** Small mason jar (250-500ml wide-mouth), starter fills 1/3 to 2/3
- **NOT:** Bread dough (too solid), soap bubbles (too uniform/large), yogurt (too liquid), slime (too transparent)

Sources: [The Perfect Loaf](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/the-best-jar-for-your-sourdough-starter/), [My Daily Sourdough Bread](https://www.mydailysourdoughbread.com/what-should-sourdough-starter-look-like/), [The Pantry Mama](https://www.pantrymama.com/how-do-you-know-when-your-starter-is-ready-to-bake-with/)
